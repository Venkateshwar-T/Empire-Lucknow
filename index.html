The Empire Way

“Built around slow evenings, bold flavours, and tables that stay occupied.”

01 — INGREDIENTS

Purity & Spices

Locally sourced seasonal produce, cold-pressed mustard oils, Kashmiri saffron threads, and hand-ground spices selected from generational purveyors across the Awadh valley.

02 — FIRE

Charcoal & Dum

High-temperature tandoor clay ovens, hand-fanned wood charcoal, and sealed earthen vessels for slow dum-cooking that transforms patience into extraordinary depth.

03 — TABLE

Dramatic Hospitality

A dining room sculpted around unhurried conversation. Heavy linen, warm amber lamps, rare vinyl acoustics, and attentive service that knows when to linger and when to withdraw.

The Night Transformation

After Dark at Empire

As the clock strikes eight, the room undergoes a deliberate transformation. The chandeliers dim, vinyl turntables spin rare jazz and low-tempo soul, the bar crafts hand-carved ice cocktails, and private salons awaken.

The Ethos
“We do not roast coffee to rush your day. We roast to give you back your evenings and late hours.”
— Kabir Saxena, Founder & Head Roaster
01

Direct Micro-Lot Ethics

We buy directly from smallholder farms in Chikmagalur, Yirgacheffe, and Boquete at double Fair Trade rates to preserve heirloom varietals.

02

No Algorithmic Audio

Every track played in Moonlight is pressed on physical analog vinyl. We believe the crackle and warmth of a needle elevates flavor.

03

The 2:00 AM Open Door

Great cities need late-night cultural sanctuaries that don't serve alcohol. Moonlight is your quiet haven long after the rest of Lucknow sleeps.

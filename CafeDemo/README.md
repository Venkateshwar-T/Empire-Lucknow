# Moonlight Cafe — Lucknow
### Artisan Micro-Lot Roastery & Late-Night Vinyl Sanctuary

> A calm, nocturnal digital experience for **Moonlight Cafe**, an artisanal specialty coffeehouse, bakery, and analog vinyl lounge located on Riverside Walk, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow. Open daily from 7:00 AM to 2:00 AM.

---

## Aesthetic & Design Philosophy

Where **EMPIRE** channels high-fashion hospitality with dramatic black/ivory contrast, **Moonlight Cafe** is designed as a tranquil nocturnal retreat:
- **Palette**: Velvety twilight ink (`#090C13`), deep night slate (`#151B2A`), glowing lunar gold (`#E2C08D`), misty blue (`#8CA0B8`), and soft warm oat milk (`#F5EFE6`).
- **Typography**: 
  - **Italiana**: Tall, ethereal celestial roman display for titles and branding.
  - **Fraunces**: Warm, tactile, organic editorial serif for dish titles and quotes.
  - **Outfit**: Modern, clean geometric sans for UI and readability.
  - **Space Grotesk**: Architectural mono for elevation tags, timestamps, and origin lots.

## Key Features

- **Hero Sanctuary**: Arched window imagery paired with interactive floating widgets:
  - **Live Vinyl Player**: Interactive turntable card spinning Bill Evans analog jazz.
  - **Today's Slow Bar Single Origin**: Ethiopia Yirgacheffe G1 (1,950m).
- **Brews by the Hour (Interactive Chronology)**:
  - ☀️ 08:00 AM — First Pour (Bright V60 & Cardamom Bun)
  - ☕ 13:30 PM — Afternoon Focus (Panama Geisha & Truffle Tartine)
  - 🌅 18:30 PM — Twilight Hour (Matcha Cloud & Pistachio Knot)
  - 🌙 23:00 PM — Midnight Sanctuary (Kyoto Cold Drip & Basque Miso Cheesecake)
- **18-Item Curated Menu & Live Search**:
  - Filter by category: Espresso & Lattes, Slow Pour-Over, Artisanal Bakery, Botanical Elixirs, Savory Tartines.
  - Instant live search bar with zero inner focus borders.
  - **Tasting Notes Inspector**: Modal with altitude, varietal, extraction process, and tasting notes.
- **The 4 Ambient Nooks**:
  - 01. The Vinyl Mezzanine
  - 02. The Glasshouse Solarium
  - 03. The Solo Library (Quiet policy & reading lamps)
  - 04. The Copper Slow Bar
- **Weekly Night Sessions Schedule**:
  - Analog Vinyl Nights, Midnight Acoustic Strings, Lo-Fi Poetry, and Sunday Cupping Workshops.
- **Interactive Nook Reservation System**:
  - Pick specific nook, party size, date, and late night time slots up to 1:00 AM.
  - Instant modal voucher confirmation with reference `#MLC-XXXX`.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Layout**:
  - Full-screen animated mobile menu drawer.
  - Glassmorphic bottom dock (4 balanced icons: Brews, Nooks, Search, Reserve).
  - Dedicated mobile search overlay with integrated `✕` close button.

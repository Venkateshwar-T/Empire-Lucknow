# Moonlight Cafe — Lucknow
### Specialty Coffee, Artisanal Bakery & Late-Night Sanctuary

> A calm, nocturnal digital experience for **Moonlight Cafe**, located on Riverside Walk, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow. Open daily from 7:00 AM to 2:00 AM.

---

## Design Highlights & Architecture

- **Branding**: Clean, elegant **Moonlight Cafe** typography with celestial crescent glyph.
- **Streamlined Navigation (No Hamburger Bloat)**:
  - **Mobile**: Simple, uncluttered header containing just the **Moonlight Cafe** wordmark, quick search trigger, and a direct **Book Table** button. 
  - **Mobile Dock**: Fixed 4-icon bottom bar for instant, one-thumb access: `Brews` (Menu), `Spaces` (Seating Areas), `Search`, and `Book Table`.
  - **Desktop**: 4 clean, non-wrapping navigation links (`Brews & Bakery`, `Seating Areas`, `Hours & Location`, `Book a Table`), quick search, and glowing reservation pill.
- **Bespoke Hero Section**:
  - Replaced generic template buttons with an **Artistic Action Deck**:
    - **Pass 01**: *The Night Brews* interactive tasting flight card (`→`).
    - **Pass 02**: *Book a Table* reservation pass (`↗`).
  - Arched celestial window photograph with warm lighting and live origin lot badge.
- **The Curated Menu (Mobile 2-Column Grid)**:
  - **Mobile 2-Column Layout**: Proportional 2-column card grid designed specifically for phones.
  - **Zero-Overflow Responsive Filters**: Smooth touch-scrollable category pills (`All Items`, `Espresso`, `Slow Pour`, `Bakery`, `Botanicals`, `Tartines`) that never expand mobile viewport widths.
  - **Instant Search**: Clean search box with clear button (`✕`) and live item filtering.
  - **Tasting Notes Inspector**: Tap any item to inspect origin, roast, and tasting notes in a modal.
- **Explore Our Seating Areas (Clear, Intuitive Hospitality Terms)**:
  - *Area 01 — Vinyl Lounge* (Vintage tube amps & velvet sofas)
  - *Area 02 — Glasshouse Solarium* (Tropical plants under starlight)
  - *Area 03 — Quiet Study Library* (Brass lamps & solo focus tables)
  - *Area 04 — Coffee Bar* (Front-row brew rituals)
  - Tapping any area smoothly scrolls to the reservation form with that seating area pre-selected.
- **Table Reservations**:
  - Select seating area, date, guest count, and time slots up to 11:30 PM.
  - Generates instant reference confirmation `#MLC-XXXX`.
- **Riverside Walk Location Card**:
  - Stylized dark vector map of Gomti Nagar with river curves, 300 Mbps fiber notice, and valet parking.
